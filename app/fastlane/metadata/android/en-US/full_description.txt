Solea brings every connected device in your house or apartment together in one simple app, in French, English and Arabic.

• Lights, plugs and breakers: switch on, off and dim with one tap.
• Curtains and shutters: open, stop, close, room by room.
• Smart locks: unlock over Bluetooth or remotely, temporary access codes for guests, entry history.
• IP cameras: keep an eye on your home.
• Scenes: "Leaving", "Good night"… several devices with a single button.
• Automations: based on time, a sensor or another device.
• Rooms and favourites: find what you use most right away.
• Outdoor weather for your city on the home screen.
• Share your home with your family, each with their own account.

Solea works with devices from several brands, installed by your Solea installer or by yourself.

Your data stays yours: no ads, no reselling. You can delete your account at any time from the app.
